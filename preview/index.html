    Fuel estimate calibration
    15.4 US gal · 4 × 440 cc/min. Confirmed: 2 squirts, alternating, four-stroke = 0.5 pulses/rev. Verify bank split and dead time.
    
        
    Known fuel in the tank
    Until CAN fuel level is available, enter the amount currently in your tank. Estimated use is subtracted while the dash records.
    Reconfirm after a service restart or missing fuel data. Fresh CAN fuel level takes priority. Range learns after at least 0.62 mi with fuel data.
    Four-stroke batch fire: pulses/rev = squirts per cycle ÷ 2 (simultaneous), or ÷ 4 (alternating). PW2 count is the number of injectors represented by bank 2.
